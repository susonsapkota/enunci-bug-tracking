# Enunci support

Need help with Enunci, your account, pronunciation practice, or a subscription? Email [hi@susonsapkota.com](mailto:hi@susonsapkota.com). Include a short description of the issue and your app version; please do not send passwords or payment details.

You can also report a bug or suggest an improvement in the app under **Settings → Send feedback**.

For subscription changes or cancellation, use **Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions** on your Apple device. For purchase-access problems, contact us at the address above.
